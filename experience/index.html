01 / Architecture & delivery

Work summary

  • Enterprise imaging architecture

    Designed target-state environments for PACS, vendor-neutral archives, enterprise viewers, and reporting systems. Mapped dependencies across clinical workflows, applications, and infrastructure.

  • PACS modernization & migration

    Led technical work on PACS and viewer migrations and upgrades, from configuration and workflow validation through cutover and post-go-live stabilization.

  • AI application integration with PACS

    Integrated AI applications into imaging workflows using DICOM routing, clinical context exchange, and worklist configuration. Validated how results were surfaced to readers and downstream applications.

  • DICOM & HL7 interoperability

    Designed and troubleshot image routing, storage, Query/Retrieve, modality worklists, and the interfaces that carry orders and results. Supported secure image exchange between organizations.

  • Radiology ordering & reporting

    Connected PACS, EHRs, and PowerScribe workflows. Validated order-to-exam matching, report delivery, and the transition from acquisition to reading.

  • Cardiovascular imaging workflows

    Designed and supported workflows connecting cardiology systems, DICOM routers, archives, and cardiovascular imaging applications. Automated image routing and reconciled study identifiers across systems.

  • Cloud migration & operational readiness

    Supported migration of PACS application components to Microsoft Azure by mapping dependencies, validating connectivity, and coordinating testing and readiness.

  • Clinical continuity & production reliability

    Validated contingency workflows and aligned configurations across environments. Traced issues with study access, performance, and integration across application, infrastructure, and vendor boundaries.
